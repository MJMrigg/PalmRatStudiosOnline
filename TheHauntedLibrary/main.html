Welcome to The Haunted Library!

In this game, you play as a college student studying in the library during finals week.
However, you neglected to remember that when the clock strikes midnight, the spirits of the library awaken!
Now, you must escape the library, and quickly. The spirits are restless and will hurt you if you let them.
Luckily, you remembered to bring your magic wand when you left your dorm. So if the spirits do get too close, you can shoot them with an Arcane Missile!
Find the exit and hurry! The spirits are coming!

How to play:
Use W and S to move forwards and backwards.
Use A and D to rotate the camera left and right.
Use Q and E to move left and right.
Left click to shoot and Arcane Missle.
Press Enter to restart the game at any time.
Get to the exit before the sprits get you.
If the spirits get you, you die and the game resets.
There are three kinds of spirits.
Wizard Ghosts shoot magic fireballs at you. If they hit you, you will die.
Plasma Ghosts are ghosts made of slime and plasma. If they touch you, you will die.
However, they have a tendency to get stuck between walls and are very slow.
Bats are not spirits. However, they will still bite you and you can die from their bites.
They are small, fast, and very sporadic with their movements. Be very careful if see one.
