Welcome to the Spooky Forest!(Wooooooo.....)

In this game, the ancient prophesy has come true. On a dark night, the aliens returned and began are abducting people left and right. Luckily, your ancestors knew this would happen and thus create the SACRED SAFETY STONE, a stone that protects all those around it from ufo attacks.
You know it's in nearby forest somewhere, you just have to find it. You'll recognize it by it's unique purple color. If you find it, you will be safe.
However, the aliens know you're the forest somewhere, and are looking for you. You must avoid being abducted at all cost.

How to play:
Use W and S to move forwards and backwards.
Use A and D to rotate left and right.
Use Q and E to move left and right.
Navigate through the forest and find the sacred safety stone. Once you touch it, you win the game.
If you the ufo's spotlight touches you, you die and the game resets.
If you at any time want to restart the game, press enter.
The difference between resetting and restarting is that reseting only restarts the player.
Restarting restarts the whole game.
