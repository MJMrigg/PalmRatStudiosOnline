Welcome to Asteriod Shooter!

In this game, you play as a spaceship pilot, tasked with shooting a whole field of asteriods so that the scavengers can come and fetch the bits. However, there are a lot of them. As such, you built in the latest gadget in radar technology, the Detectotron 9000, perfect for scanning entire asteriod fields!
With this installed in your spaceship, you have it update your viewport every time you blow up and asteriod.
With that installed, you head out to the asteroid field, ready to blow stuff up!

How to play:
Use W and S to move forward and backward.
Use A and D to rotate your ship to the left and right.
Use Q and E or Z and X to move your ship up or down.
Left click to shoot a bullet.
Shoot bullets at asteroids to damage them.
Asteroids have 3 health and bullets do 1 damage, so you'll have to shoot it 3 times.
There are 200 asteroids. Once you destroy all of them, you win!
To restart the game at any time, press enter.
There is also a cheat code involving another key on your keyboard. Try to find it!
